Kajian Yuridis dan Regulasi Kecerdasan Buatan (Artificial Intelligence) dalam Perlindungan Data Pribadi di Indonesia Legal Analysis and Regulation of Artificial Intelligence in Personal Data Protection in Indonesia 印度尼西亚人工智能与个人数据保护的法律审查及监管研究

Dr. Ilham Pradani C.L. Chain Asia Connect Scientific Journal Vol. 3 No. 1 (2026)

Abstrak: Abstract: 摘要: Pesatnya adopsi model generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) dan Large Language Models (LLM) di sektor korporasi maupun publik memicu disrupsi regulasi perlindungan data pribadi. Penelitian ini menganalisis secara mendalam harmonisasi prinsip data minimization, purpose limitation, dan algorithmic transparency berdasarkan Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Perlindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP). Disertai kajian komparatif terhadap EU AI Act untuk merumuskan kerangka pertanggungjawaban hukum (liability framework) bagi pengembang dan pengendali data AI di Indonesia. The rapid adoption of generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Large Language Models (LLMs) across corporate and public sectors introduces regulatory disruptions to personal data protection. This study provides a comprehensive legal analysis harmonizing data minimization, purpose limitation, and algorithmic transparency under the Indonesian Personal Data Protection Act (PDP Law No. 27/2022). It includes a comparative study with the EU AI Act to formulate a robust liability framework for AI developers and data controllers in Indonesia. 企业与公共部门对生成式人工智能（AI）及大语言模型（LLM）的迅速采纳对个人数据保护监管构成了实质性挑战。本研究基于印度尼西亚《个人数据保护法》（UU PDP 2022年第27号），深入剖析了数据最小化、目的限制与算法透明度原则的法学协调机制，并结合欧盟《人工智能法案》（EU AI Act）展开比较法学分析，构建印尼AI开发者与数据控制者的法定责任框架。

Pertanggungjawaban Hukum Pengembang Model Algoritma Trading Terdesentralisasi (DeFi & Algorithmic Trading) Legal Liability of Developers in Decentralized Algorithmic Trading Models (DeFi & Quantitative Execution) 去中心化算法交易模型（DeFi与量化交易）开发者的法律责任界定

Dr. Ilham Pradani C.L. Chain Asia Connect Scientific Journal Vol. 3 No. 2 (2026)

Abstrak: Abstract: 摘要: Otomasi trading menggunakan model ensemble machine learning dan smart contracts di decentralized exchanges memunculkan problematika yurisdiksi dan pertanggungjawaban hukum saat terjadi flash crashes atau eksploitasi kode. Penelitian ini mengkaji doktrin fault-based liability vs strict liability, perlindungan hukum investor ritel, serta kewajiban kepatuhan audit keamanan kontrak pintar menurut hukum perdata dan regulasi pasar komoditi berjangka. Trading automation using machine learning ensembles and smart contracts on decentralized exchanges presents complex jurisdictional issues and legal liability when algorithmic flash crashes or code exploits occur. This research examines fault-based vs strict liability doctrines, retail investor safeguards, and mandatory smart contract security audit requirements under civil law and commodity futures trading regulations. 在去中心化交易所中应用机器学习集成算法与智能合约实现交易自动化，在发生闪崩或代码漏洞利用时引发了复杂的司法管辖权与法定赔偿问题。本研究围绕过错责任与严格责任学说、散户投资者合法权益保护，以及民商法与大宗商品期货监管项下智能合约强制安全审计要求展开论证。

Keabsahan Pembuktian Bukti Digital (Digital Evidence) dan Integritas Audit Forensik Siber Menurut Hukum Positif Admissibility of Digital Evidence and Forensic Audit Integrity under Statutory Positive Law 法定证据体系下电子数字证据（Digital Evidence）有效性与网络取证审计完整性研究

Dr. Ilham Pradani C.L. Chain Asia Connect Scientific Journal Vol. 2 No. 3 (2025)

Abstrak: Abstract: 摘要: Bukti elektronik memiliki karakteristik mudah dimanipulasi (*volatile*) sehingga memerlukan prosedur perolehan yang memenuhi kriteria *admissibility* di hadapan majelis hakim. Riset ini menelaah keterpenuhan Pasal 5 dan Pasal 6 UU ITE mengenai keabsahan dokumen elektronik, validitas nilai hash kriptografis (SHA-256), tata laksana *chain of custody*, serta sertifikasi kompetensi auditor forensik digital dalam pengungkapan insiden peretasan infrastruktur siber. Electronic evidence is inherently volatile and susceptible to alteration, requiring strict chain-of-custody procedures to meet courtroom admissibility standards. This research evaluates statutory requirements under Articles 5 and 6 of the Indonesian ITE Law concerning electronic document validity, SHA-256 cryptographic hash integrity, evidence handling protocols, and digital forensic auditor certifications during enterprise cybersecurity breach litigation. 电子证据具有易损与易篡改特性，因此需要严格的取证程序以符合法庭证据可采性标准。本论文对印尼《电子信息与交易法》（UU ITE）第5条和第6条关于电子文件的法律效力、SHA-256密码学哈希完整性、电子数据羁押链规程以及网络安全取证审计师资质认定进行了系统性审查。

Strategic Case Study: Transformasi Digital, Arsitektur ERP Terintegrasi, dan Kesempurnaan Operasional Perusahaan Strategic Case Study: Digital Transformation, Integrated ERP Architecture, and Enterprise Operational Excellence 战略案例研究：数字化转型、集成式ERP系统架构与企业卓越运营

Dr. Ilham Pradani C.L. Chain Asia Connect Scientific Journal Vol. 3 No. 1 (2026)

Abstrak: Abstract: 摘要: Makalah studi kasus terapan ini membahas rekayasa arsitektur sistem enterprise pada ekosistem manufaktur dan distribusi logistik. Menyajikan bukti empiris penghematan biaya operasional, reduksi redundansi data pergudangan sebesar 40%, serta pemenuhan SLA ketersediaan sistem 99,9% melalui integrasi database terdistribusi dan kepatuhan terhadap audit tata kelola TI. This applied case study investigates enterprise system architecture engineering across manufacturing ecosystems and supply chain logistics. It presents empirical proof of operational cost savings, a 40% reduction in warehouse inventory data redundancies, and 99.9% high-availability SLA compliance achieved through distributed database clustering and IT governance audit frameworks. 本篇实证案例论文深入探讨了大型制造与物流配送生态下的企业级ERP系统架构工程。通过分布式数据库集群与IT治理审计合规，实现了运营成本显著节约、仓储冗余数据消减40%，以及保障99.9%高可用SLA系统稳定运行的实证结果。
